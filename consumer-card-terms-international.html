The Card is a spend‑from‑balance product. Your ability to transact is limited to the available balance funded into your Card account via supported stablecoins (e.g., USDC, USDT) and networks. Authorizations may include temporary holds (e.g., fuel pumps, hotels) that reduce your available balance until final settlement. Autospend, the Issuer, the Program Manager, or the Card Network may set per‑transaction or aggregate limits and may change limits at any time to manage risk or comply with law.

You may use your Card to purchase goods and services at merchants that accept your Card Network. Cash advances and balance transfers are not available. 7. Currency Conversion & International Transactions

If you transact in a currency other than USD, the Card Network converts the transaction to USD using the rate selected by the network on the processing date. A network FX fee (up to 2%) may apply, and an international processing fee of $0.30 per transaction may apply. Merchants or third parties may perform their own conversions at rates they select before submitting to the network.

8. Disputes, Chargebacks & Fees
